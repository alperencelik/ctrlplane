A Kubernetes API of its own, with its own endpoint and certificate authority. It's ready in about a minute.

Get started

  1. Create a control plane. Your own Kubernetes API, with its own endpoint.
  2. Connect. ctrlplane use <name>, or download its kubeconfig.
  3. Add your APIs. Apply your CRDs, in the API tab or with kubectl apply.
  4. Run your controllers. Any image, run for you next to its API{{if .Limits.Tunnel}}, reaching your network through a tunnel{{end}}.
